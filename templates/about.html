City Library is dedicated to intellectual freedom, digital equity, and fostering a lifelong passion for reading, exploration, and community engagement.

Our Mission

Empowering Our Community with Equitable Access to Knowledge

For over a century, City Library has served as the intellectual sanctuary of our metropolitan area. From physical archival manuscripts to modern online lending, our mission remains steadfast: connecting every citizen with information, ideas, and each other.

We believe that libraries are not merely warehouses for books, but dynamic spaces of discovery, collaboration, innovation, and personal transformation.
